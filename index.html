SMPLR Monotone 1

2026-03-18 | 17 songs | 95 minutes | one algorithm Listen for free on YouTube The SMPLR compositional algorithm remains unchanged. The only difference is the sound library. The result is a less chaotic, more harmonious listening experience that – in this instance - evokes mantras & meditative temples. Monotone 1 ignores the 13,000 audio file sample library used for The Infinite Album live stream, and relies on just a small handful of closely related source sounds... 3, in fact. These 3 sounds are batch processed using FFAB against several hundred convolution IR reverb files to create a subtle sonic palette of ~2869 nearly identical samples: Same sound, different spaces.
